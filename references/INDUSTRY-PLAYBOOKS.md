# Industry Playbooks: 16 Businesses

For each industry: what customers really buy (the result), the proof that works best, the before and after, and the best next step. Use these as starting ideas only. Claim only what is true for the business you are rewriting.

## 1. Dentist or clinic
- **They really buy:** no pain, a smile they're proud of, not being judged, speed.
- **Best proof:** patient count, Google rating with number of reviews, a review about gentleness, qualifications.
- **Before and after:** "You put off the visit for months" / "In and out in 40 minutes, and it didn't hurt."
- **Next step:** "Book a free 15 minute check up."
- **Careful:** no outcome guarantees, check local rules on before and after photos.

## 2. Accountant or bookkeeper
- **They really buy:** never thinking about books, no tax panic, no fines, knowing their numbers.
- **Best proof:** number of businesses served, a "saved me X" review, on time filing record.
- **Before and after:** "A weekend of receipts every March" / "1 call in March, and it's filed."
- **Next step:** "Reply YES for a fixed monthly price for your business."

## 3. Restaurant or cafe
- **They really buy:** a good time, no waiting, the one dish they'll tell friends about.
- **Best proof:** the signature dish with a specific detail, rating and review count, years open.
- **Before and after:** "40 minutes in a queue on Friday" / "Book on WhatsApp, walk straight in."
- **Next step:** "Book a table on WhatsApp" or "Order ahead and skip the line."

## 4. Salon, spa or barber
- **They really buy:** looking good for an event, feeling looked after, a stylist who gets it.
- **Best proof:** before and after photos (with permission), rebooking rate, reviews naming a stylist.
- **Before and after:** "You show a photo and hope" / "You leave looking like the photo."
- **Next step:** "Book your slot: [link]. Free fringe trims between visits." (only if true)

## 5. Gym or fitness coach
- **They really buy:** actually showing up, visible progress, not feeling lost.
- **Best proof:** member count, a member's real result with permission, attendance support you really give.
- **Before and after:** "You pay every month and go twice" / "Your coach texts if you miss your 7 am."
- **Next step:** "Book a free first session."
- **Careful:** no weight loss or health guarantees.

## 6. Home repair (plumber, electrician, painter, cleaner)
- **They really buy:** it's fixed today, a fair price, no mess, trust in their home.
- **Best proof:** response time, jobs done, before and after photos, a guarantee you honour, verified ID.
- **Before and after:** "A leak on Sunday night and 6 numbers that don't answer" / "Fixed by 10 am, floor wiped."
- **Next step:** "Send a photo and your area for an exact price today."

## 7. Real estate agent
- **They really buy:** the right home faster, a fair price, fewer wasted viewings, a quick sale.
- **Best proof:** homes sold in the area, average days to sell (real), a client review.
- **Before and after:** "15 viewings of homes that looked better online" / "3 viewings, all matching your list."
- **Next step:** "Send your must have list and budget; I'll reply with 3 matches by tomorrow."

## 8. Marketing or design agency
- **They really buy:** more customers, a predictable pipeline, not managing freelancers.
- **Best proof:** a named client result with permission, the numbers you track, the report they get.
- **Before and after:** "Visitors but no enquiries" / "Booked calls every week, with a report showing where each came from."
- **Next step:** "Send your website; I'll reply with 3 fixes, free."
- **Careful:** never promise a specific growth figure.

## 9. Software or SaaS
- **They really buy:** time back, fewer errors, looking good to the boss, not learning a new tool.
- **Best proof:** a customer's time or money saved, setup time, number of customers, a named logo with permission.
- **Before and after:** "Copying data between 4 tools every morning" / "The data moves itself."
- **Next step:** "Start free: [link]. Set up in 10 minutes." (only if true)

## 10. Coach or consultant
- **They really buy:** clarity, a specific outcome, someone in their corner.
- **Best proof:** a client story with permission, the method in 3 steps, credentials.
- **Before and after:** "Busy every day, no idea if it's working" / "1 page plan, 3 numbers, weekly check in."
- **Next step:** "Book a free 20 minute call; you'll leave with your top 3 priorities."

## 11. Tutor or school
- **They really buy:** a confident child, better marks, calmer evenings.
- **Best proof:** years teaching, student count, a parent's review, the method.
- **Before and after:** "Homework ends in tears" / "Your child asks for the next sheet."
- **Next step:** "Book a free trial class."
- **Careful:** no guaranteed marks.

## 12. E commerce shop
- **They really buy:** the product working as promised, fast delivery, easy returns.
- **Best proof:** reviews with photos, units sold, delivery time, return policy.
- **Before and after:** "A phone case that cracks in a month" / "Dropped 30 times, still fine." (only if tested)
- **Next step:** "Order today, delivered by Friday. Free returns for 30 days." (only if true)

## 13. Lawyer or legal service
- **They really buy:** a clear answer, a plan, not being alone, cost certainty.
- **Best proof:** years of practice, areas handled, fixed fee options, a client review (where allowed).
- **Before and after:** "A letter you don't understand and a deadline" / "A plain explanation and a plan by Friday."
- **Next step:** "Book a 20 minute first call."
- **Careful:** follow your bar's advertising rules; never promise outcomes.

## 14. Event planner or wedding vendor
- **They really buy:** a perfect day without stress, the photos, guests talking about it.
- **Best proof:** events done, real photos, a couple's review, a day of timeline sample.
- **Before and after:** "200 calls with 20 vendors" / "1 WhatsApp group, 1 person handling it."
- **Next step:** "Send your date and guest count for a free plan."

## 15. Manufacturer or B2B supplier
- **They really buy:** reliable delivery, consistent quality, no line stoppages.
- **Best proof:** on time delivery rate, years supplying, certifications, client count, defect rate.
- **Before and after:** "A late batch stops your line for a day" / "Delivery on the agreed date, with a tracking update every day."
- **Next step:** "Send your spec sheet; we'll quote within 24 hours."

## 16. Freelancer (writer, developer, designer, editor)
- **They really buy:** it done right the first time, on time, without managing them.
- **Best proof:** portfolio pieces with results, client reviews, turnaround time.
- **Before and after:** "3 rounds of edits and still not right" / "First draft in 48 hours, usually approved in 1 round."
- **Next step:** "Send your brief; I'll reply with a price and a date today."
