# Next Step Library

Every piece of text ends with exactly one next step. A good next step is:

- **Specific:** the reader knows exactly what to do.
- **Small:** low effort and low risk (seconds, not a commitment).
- **Clear about what happens next:** "You'll get a price and a start date."
- **Matched to temperature:** a stranger needs a smaller step than someone who already asked for a quote.

## Formula

```
[Action verb] [specific thing]. [What they get or what happens next].
```

## By situation

### Cold (they don't know you yet)
1. "Reply YES and I'll send you the 1 page plan for your shop."
2. "Want the free checklist we use? Reply 'list' and it's yours."
3. "Send me your website link and I'll reply with 3 quick fixes, free."
4. "Is it worth a 10 minute call on Thursday? Reply with a time."
5. "Get the free price guide: [link]. No sign up."

### Warm (they visited, enquired, or know you)
6. "Book a free 15 minute call: [link]. You'll leave with a price and a start date."
7. "Send a photo of the problem and your area. I'll reply with the exact price today."
8. "Pick a time that suits you: [link]. Takes 30 seconds."
9. "Reply with your budget and date, and I'll send 2 options by tonight."
10. "Tap here to see 3 kitchens we did in your area: [link]."

### Hot (ready to buy)
11. "Reply YES and I'll send the invoice and lock your date."
12. "Pay the 20% deposit here to confirm your slot: [link]."
13. "Book your first session: [link]. You can reschedule free up to 24 hours before."
14. "Order on WhatsApp: [number]. Delivered by 6 pm today."
15. "Start your free 14 day trial: [link]. No card needed." (only if true)

### Local and walk in
16. "Walk in any time before 9 pm. No booking needed."
17. "Call now, we pick up in 3 rings: [number]."
18. "Get directions: [map link]. Free parking at the back."
19. "Show this message at the counter for your free coffee." (only if real)

### Follow up (they went quiet)
20. "Should I hold your date for 2 more days, or let it go?"
21. "Quick yes or no: still interested in [result]?"
22. "I found 1 more idea for [their goal]. Want it?"
23. "Is now a bad time? Reply 'later' and I'll check back in a month."

### Content and social
24. "Comment 'GUIDE' and I'll send it to you."
25. "Save this for the next time [situation happens]."
26. "Send this to the person in your team who does [task]."

### Proposals and quotes
27. "Reply 'approved' and we start on Monday."
28. "Pick option A or B and I'll send the agreement today."
29. "Book 20 minutes to go through it together: [link]."
30. "This price holds until [real date], because [real reason]."

## Rules

- **Exactly one.** If there are 2 good actions, pick the one closest to a sale. Put the other one nowhere.
- **No fake urgency.** Only use a deadline if it's real ("Slots for October: 3 left" only if true).
- **Remove the risk.** Free, no sign up, cancel any time, reschedule free: say it if true.
- **Make it physical.** "Tap", "reply", "send a photo" beat "get in touch".
- **Repeat it.** On a long page, the same one step can appear at the top and the bottom. Same step, not a different one.
