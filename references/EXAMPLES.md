# Examples: 6 Before and After Rewrites

All businesses and numbers below are examples made up to show the method. In real use, only the person's own facts are used.

## 1. Dentist (website homepage)

**Before**
> Welcome to Smile Dental Clinic. We offer high quality dental care with state of the art equipment and a patient first approach. Our experienced team provides a wide range of services. Feel free to contact us for more information.

**After**
> **Walk out with a whiter smile in 1 visit.**
> Before: you hide your teeth in photos. After: you smile without thinking about it.
> 1,200+ patients treated since 2015. "I finally smile in photos again." Neha R., Google review
> **Book a free 15 minute check up.**

## 2. Accountant (sales email)

**Before**
> Dear Sir, we are a leading accounting firm providing end to end bookkeeping, tax and compliance solutions for SMEs. We would love to discuss how we can help your business. Please don't hesitate to reach out.

**After**
> Subject: Your books, done every month without you touching them
>
> Hi Arjun,
> Right now tax season probably means a weekend of receipts. With us, you send a photo of each bill on WhatsApp, and your books are ready every month.
> We do this for 85 small businesses in Pune. [PROOF NEEDED: one client quote about saved time]
> Reply YES and I'll send you a fixed monthly price for your business.

## 3. Cafe (Google Business profile)

**Before**
> Cozy cafe offering a variety of beverages and snacks in a relaxing ambience. Best coffee in town. Free WiFi.

**After**
> Fresh coffee, ready in 3 minutes, with a quiet table and fast WiFi to work for hours.
> Beans roasted in Bengaluru every week. 4.7 stars from 640 Google reviews.
> Open 7 am to 10 pm. Order ahead on WhatsApp and skip the line.

## 4. Marketing agency (proposal opener)

**Before**
> Thank you for the opportunity. XYZ Digital is a full service digital marketing agency with a passion for innovative, data driven strategies tailored to your unique needs.

**After**
> In 90 days, you should be getting booked calls from your website every week, not just visitors.
> Today: about 2,000 visits a month and 3 enquiries. Our goal for you: 15 enquiries a month, with a weekly report showing exactly where each one came from.
> For a similar clinic last year, we went from 4 to 19 enquiries a month in 4 months. [PROOF NEEDED: permission to name the clinic]
> Next step: a 30 minute call to agree the 3 numbers we'll track.

## 5. Software tool (pitch, 2 sentences)

**Before**
> We're an AI powered platform that leverages cutting edge technology to streamline workflows and empower teams.

**After**
> Your team stops copying data between spreadsheets: every new order appears in your stock sheet and invoice in under a minute. One customer saved 6 hours a week in their first month. [PROOF NEEDED: confirm the 6 hours with the customer]

## 6. Home repair (WhatsApp reply to a price question)

**Before**
> Hello sir, thanks for contacting us. We provide all types of plumbing services at affordable prices. Please share your requirement and we will get back to you.

**After**
> Hi! For a leaking tap, most fixes are 400 to 700 rupees and take under an hour. Send me a photo of the tap and your area, and I'll give you the exact price and the earliest time today.
