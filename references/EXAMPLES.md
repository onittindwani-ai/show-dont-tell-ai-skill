# Examples: 12 Before and After Rewrites

Every business, name and number below is a made up example to show the method. In real use, only the person's own facts and proof are used. Missing proof is marked `[PROOF NEEDED]`, never invented.

## 1. Dentist (website homepage)

**Before**
> Welcome to Smile Dental Clinic. We offer high quality dental care with state of the art equipment and a patient first approach. Our experienced team provides a wide range of services. Feel free to contact us for more information.

**After**
> **Walk out with a whiter smile in 1 visit.**
> Gentle check ups, cleaning and whitening in Pune, with early and late appointments.
> Before: you hide your teeth in photos. After: you smile without thinking about it.
> 1,200+ patients treated since 2015. "I was scared of dentists. I didn't feel a thing." Neha R., Google review
> **Book a free 15 minute check up. You'll know what you need and the price before you leave.**

**Score:** 4 to 27 out of 30. **Why it works:** the first line is a picture, the review answers the biggest fear (pain), and the step removes the second fear (surprise costs).

## 2. Accountant (cold sales email)

**Before**
> Dear Sir, I hope this email finds you well. We are a leading accounting firm providing end to end bookkeeping, tax and compliance solutions for SMEs. We would love to discuss how we can help your business. Please don't hesitate to reach out.

**After**
> Subject: Your books, done every month without you touching them
>
> Hi Arjun,
> Right now tax season probably means a weekend of receipts. With us, you send a photo of each bill on WhatsApp, and your books are ready by the 5th of every month.
> We do this for 85 small businesses in Pune. [PROOF NEEDED: 1 client quote about time saved]
> Reply YES and I'll send you a fixed monthly price for your business.

**Score:** 3 to 25. **Why it works:** it describes his Sunday, not their firm, and the reply takes 3 letters.

## 4. Marketing agency (proposal opener)

**Before**
> Thank you for the opportunity. XYZ Digital is a full service digital marketing agency with a passion for innovative, data driven strategies tailored to your unique needs.

**After**
> In 90 days, your website should be bringing you booked calls every week, not just visitors.
> Today: about 2,000 visits a month and 3 enquiries. Our goal for you: 15 enquiries a month, with a weekly report showing where each one came from.
> For a similar clinic last year, enquiries went from 4 to 19 a month in 4 months. [PROOF NEEDED: permission to name the clinic]
> Next step: book a 30 minute call to agree the 3 numbers we'll track.

**Score:** 5 to 26. **Why it works:** it opens with the client's numbers, labels the 15 as a goal (not a promise), and proves it with a real past result.
