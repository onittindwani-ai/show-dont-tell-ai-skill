# 20 Headline Formulas

The headline is the first line of a page, the subject of an email, the first sentence of a reply. It decides whether anything else gets read.

**Rules for every formula:** 4 to 10 words. About the customer. Specific (a number, time, thing or moment). True for this business. No hype words ("amazing", "ultimate", "revolutionary").

Write 5 options using different formulas, then pick the one a stranger would understand fastest.

## Result formulas

| # | Formula | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Result] in [time] | "Your website, live in 7 days" |
| 2 | [Result] without [pain] | "Clean books without a weekend of receipts" |
| 3 | [Result], [proof number] | "Whiter teeth in 1 visit. 1,200 patients since 2015" |
| 4 | Get [result] by [date or event] | "Get your kitchen done before Diwali" |
| 5 | From [before] to [after] | "From 3 enquiries a month to 15" (only if it happened) |

## Pain formulas

| # | Formula | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | Stop [painful habit] | "Stop chasing clients for payments" |
| 7 | Never [bad moment] again | "Never miss a call from a customer again" |
| 8 | No more [pain] | "No more leaking taps on Sunday nights" |
| 9 | Tired of [pain]? [Fix] | "Tired of copying orders? They copy themselves now" |

## Who it's for formulas

| # | Formula | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | [Result] for [specific person] | "Bookkeeping for cafes that close at midnight" |

| # | Formula | Example |

## Proof and curiosity formulas

| # | Formula | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | [Number] [people] already [result] | "640 families already order their bread here" |
| 17 | What [customer] said after [result] | "What Priya said after her first full week of orders" |
| 18 | The [surprising fact] about [topic] | "The 2 minute check that saves most roofs" |
| 19 | Why [specific people] switch to [you] | "Why 40 local gyms switched to us this year" (only if true) |
| 20 | [Question the customer already asks] | "Is your website losing you customers at night?" |

## Sub headline (the line under the headline)

The sub headline answers "how?" or "for whom?" in 1 sentence:

- "We build, write and launch your site. You approve it in 1 call."
- "For cafes, salons and clinics in Pune."
- "Send us photos of your receipts on WhatsApp. We handle the rest."

## Headline mistakes

- Brand name as the headline ("Welcome to GreenLeaf"): move the name to the logo.
- A slogan nobody understands ("Growth, reimagined"): say the actual result.
- Too many results ("Faster, cheaper, better and easier"): pick one.
- A result you can't deliver for most customers: use what's typical, not your best ever case.
