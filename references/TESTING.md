# Testing Old Against New (Simple and Fair)

A rewrite is a guess until real customers react to it. These tests need no special software.

## Pick the right number to watch

| Text | Watch this number |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage or landing page | Enquiries, bookings or orders per 100 visitors |
| Sales email | Replies per 100 emails sent |
| DM or WhatsApp reply | Enquiries that turn into a booking or sale |
| Proposal | Proposals accepted, and days to a yes |
| Google profile | Calls, direction requests and website clicks (shown in your Google Business dashboard) |
| Pitch | How often people ask a follow up question or for your number |

## 4 fair tests

### 1. Before and after weeks (websites, profiles)
Keep the old version for 2 weeks and note the number. Switch to the new one for the next 2 weeks. Compare. Keep everything else the same (no ads change, no sale), and avoid holidays.

### 2. Split send (emails)
Send the old email to 20 people and the new one to 20 similar people on the same day. Count replies after 3 days. Repeat once before deciding.

### 3. Alternate replies (DMs, WhatsApp)
Use the old reply for odd numbered enquiries and the new one for even numbered enquiries for 2 weeks. Count bookings for each.

### 4. The 5 stranger test (anything, in 10 minutes)
Show the new version to 5 people who don't know your business for 5 seconds. Ask: "What do they sell? Why should you believe them? What would you do next?" If 4 of 5 answer all 3 correctly, it's working. Do the same with the old version to compare.

## Rules for a fair test

- Change one thing at a time when you can (the headline, or the ending).
- Compare the same kind of days and the same kind of customers.
- Small numbers lie. Wait for at least 20 to 30 actions (replies, enquiries) before deciding, or repeat the test.
- Write the result down with the dates. Next time you'll know what works for your customers.
