# Audit Scorecard (out of 30)

Use this to score any business text before and after a rewrite. 6 areas, 0 to 5 points each.

| Area | 0 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Result first** | Opens with the business name or "We are..." | The result appears, but not in the first line | The first line is a specific result for the customer |
| **2. Before and after** | No change shown | A change is described in abstract words | A concrete before and after that passes the camera test |
| **3. Proof** | No proof, or only adjectives | Some proof, but vague ("many happy clients") | Specific, real proof near the top (number, review, result) |
| **4. Next step** | None, or "contact us for more info" | A step exists but is vague or there are several | Exactly one specific, small step that says what happens next |
| **5. Plain words** | 3 or more explaining phrases, long sentences | 1 or 2 explaining phrases | None; short sentences; facts instead of adjectives |
| **6. Customer focus** | "We" far more than "you" | About even | "You" clearly more than "we"; written from the customer's side |

## Reading the score

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 10 | Explaining. Most readers leave without understanding the offer. Rewrite fully. |
| 11 to 20 | Mixed. Some value is visible but buried. Fix the first line and the ending first. |
| 21 to 26 | Showing. Strong. Add better proof to go higher. |
| 27 to 30 | Excellent. Test small changes only. |

## How to report it

```
Score: 9 / 30 (before) -> 26 / 30 (after)
Biggest fixes: result in line 1 (+5), one next step (+4), real review added (+3)
```

## The 3 fastest fixes (usually worth 10+ points)

1. Replace the first line with the customer's result.
2. Replace the ending with one specific next step.
3. Move your best real proof to just under the headline.
