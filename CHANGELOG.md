# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)

First release.

- `SKILL.md`: the 6 rules (result first, before and after, real proof only, one next step, plain words, keep it true), an 8 step method with a fixed reply format, a 10 point self check and 9 special situations.
- `PASTE-INTO-CHATGPT-OR-CLAUDE.md`: the full method as one copy and paste prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat, with follow up commands.
- `templates/`: 6 detailed templates (homepage, sales emails, proposal, enquiry replies, Google Business profile, pitch and bios), each with structure, fill in versions, line by line guidance and common mistakes.
- `references/`: 60+ explaining phrases, 20 headline formulas, a before and after guide, a 7 level proof ladder with a 2 week plan, 30 next steps, 16 industry playbooks, a 30 point scorecard, 4 fair tests and 12 full before and after examples.
- `scripts/check_copy.py`: a read only checker with no dependencies, with file, stdin, text and JSON modes and 6 built in self tests.
- `tests/`: automatic tests, including every example, run on GitHub on Python 3.9, 3.11 and 3.13.
- Tested before release (16 Oct 2026) in fresh AI chats with the paste prompt: a homepage with no proof (it marked 3 `[PROOF NEEDED]` spots and invented nothing) and a cold sales email with one real fact, 12 clients (it used only that fact, scored the email 3 to 22 out of 30, gave a review request message and a fair 100 email test, and warned to offer the free check only if it will really be done).
