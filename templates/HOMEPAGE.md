# Template: Website Homepage (or Landing Page)

The top of your homepage has one job: in 5 seconds, a stranger should know what they get, why to believe you, and what to tap. Everything else on the page supports that.

## The structure

```
1. HEADLINE          The result the customer gets. 4 to 10 words.
2. SUB HEADLINE      For whom, and how it happens. 1 sentence.
3. MAIN BUTTON       One specific next step.
4. PROOF STRIP       Your 2 or 3 strongest real proofs, right under the button.
5. BEFORE AND AFTER  2 lines, or 3 short pairs.
6. HOW IT WORKS      3 steps, from first contact to result.
7. WHAT YOU GET      What's included, as specific items, not adjectives.
8. ONE STORY         A real customer result with their words (with permission).
9. QUESTIONS         The 3 to 5 worries people have before buying, answered.
10. SAME BUTTON      The same next step again, with a risk remover.
```

## Fill in version

```
[HEADLINE: the result]
[SUB HEADLINE: who it's for + how, in 1 sentence]
[BUTTON: Book / Order / Get ... + what happens next]

[PROOF 1: a real number]  ·  [PROOF 2: rating + review count]  ·  [PROOF 3: credential or process detail]

Before: [a specific moment in the customer's life today]
After:  [the same moment, with you]

How it works
1. [First contact, made easy: "Send a photo on WhatsApp"]
2. [What you do: "We visit within 24 hours and give a written price"]
3. [The result: "Fixed the same day, area cleaned"]

What you get
- [Specific item, with a number or detail]
- [Specific item]
- [Specific item]

"[A real customer's words about the result]"
[First name, initial, business or area, source]

Questions
[Worry 1, for example "What if I'm not happy?"] [Honest answer]
[Worry 2, for example "How much does it cost?"] [A price, a range, or what it depends on]
[Worry 3, for example "How long does it take?"] [The real time]

[SAME BUTTON] [Risk remover: "Free, no obligation" / "Cancel any time" (only if true)]
```

## Line by line guidance

**1. Headline.** Use [../references/HEADLINE-FORMULAS.md](../references/HEADLINE-FORMULAS.md). Write 5, keep the clearest. Never your business name, never "Welcome", never a clever slogan.

**2. Sub headline.** Answers "for whom?" and "how?". Include the location for local businesses: people search "near me".

**3. Button.** The words on the button are the action, not "Submit" or "Learn more": "Book my free check up", "Get my price". Add one line under it saying what happens next.

**4. Proof strip.** Your 3 best real proofs, short, right under the button. If you have none yet, use process proof ("Written quote in 24 hours") and mark the rest `[PROOF NEEDED]`.

**5. Before and after.** Use [../references/BEFORE-AFTER.md](../references/BEFORE-AFTER.md). The camera test: could you film it?

**6. How it works.** Exactly 3 steps. Step 1 is always the easy first contact. Step 3 is the result.

**7. What you get.** Specific: "2 cleaners, 3 hours, supplies included" beats "comprehensive cleaning".

**8. One story.** One real customer, real words, with permission. One great story beats ten generic logos.

**9. Questions.** Write the questions people actually ask on calls or WhatsApp. The price question is the most important; answer it honestly, even with a range.

**10. Same button.** The same one action as at the top. Not a different one.

## For a short page (or a 1 screen landing page)

Use only 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5. That's enough to work.

## Common mistakes

- A slider with 5 rotating messages: pick one message.
- 6 buttons in the first screen ("Call", "Email", "Shop", "About", "Blog", "Careers"): one main button.
- Stock photos of people shaking hands: use real photos of your work, place or team.
- "Services" as the first section: start with the result; services come after.
- Hiding the price everywhere: give a starting price or range if you possibly can.
