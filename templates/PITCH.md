# Template: Elevator Pitch (spoken, 15 to 20 seconds)

```
You know how [the problem, in the listener's words]?
I help [who] get [result] in [time], without [the pain they expect].
For example, [one real client result, 1 sentence].
[One ask: "Can I show you how it would work for you?"]
```

Rules: no company history, no list of services. Say it out loud once before using it; if you run out of breath, cut words.
