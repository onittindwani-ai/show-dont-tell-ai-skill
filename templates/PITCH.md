# Template: Elevator Pitch, Intros and Bios

When someone asks "so what do you do?", most people say their job title and lose the room. A good answer makes them say "oh, how?" or "my friend needs that".

## The 20 second pitch

### Structure
```
1. THE PROBLEM     "You know how [problem, in the listener's words]?"
2. THE RESULT      "I help [who] get [result] in [time], without [the pain they expect]."
3. THE PROOF       "For example, [one real client result, 1 sentence]."
4. THE ASK         One small question or offer
```

### Fill in version
```
You know how [specific problem most people in the room recognise]?
I help [specific type of customer] [get specific result] [in a time frame], without [the hassle they expect].
For example, [real customer] went from [before] to [after] in [time].
[Ask: "Do you know anyone dealing with that?" / "Want me to show you how it'd work for you?"]
```

### Example
```
You know how most small clinics still take bookings by phone, and miss calls when they're busy?
I build simple booking systems so patients book themselves, 24 hours a day, usually live in 3 weeks.
One clinic doubled its bookings the month it launched.
Do you know a clinic owner who's always on the phone?
```

## The 5 second version (for name badges, bios, introductions)

```
I help [who] [get result].
```
"I help clinics stop missing bookings." "I help cafes get Saturday orders out on time."

## The 60 second version (for meetings, podcasts, video intros)

Add 2 things to the 20 second pitch:
- **The story:** one real customer, before and after, 2 or 3 sentences.
- **Why you:** one honest reason you're different ("I only work with clinics, so I've seen every booking problem there is").

## Bios (LinkedIn, Instagram, website about, speaker bio)

```
LINE 1   What you help people get (the result), and for whom
LINE 2   Real proof: a count, a years number, a notable result
LINE 3   One next step or what to contact you for
```

Instagram bio example:
```
Cakes that make the whole party go quiet for a second, in Jaipur.
1,100 cakes since 2020, 4.9 on Google.
Order 3 days ahead: tap WhatsApp below.
```

## Practising it

1. Say it out loud 3 times. If you run out of breath or stumble, cut words.
2. Say it to someone outside your industry. If they can't repeat it back, simplify.
3. Notice which version makes people ask "how?" Keep that one.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with your job title ("I'm a digital marketing consultant").
- Listing everything you do.
- Jargon the listener doesn't use.
- No example: the example is what people remember.
- Ending without a question.
