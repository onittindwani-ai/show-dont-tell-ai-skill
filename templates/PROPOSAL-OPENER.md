# Template: Proposal Opening (first half page)

```
In [time frame], you should have [the result, in their numbers].

Today: [where they are now, with their real numbers if you have them]
Goal: [where they'll be, with the 1 to 3 numbers you'll track]

How we'll get there: [3 steps, 1 line each]

Proof: [a similar real client result, with permission, or a process guarantee]

Next step: [one action with a date]
```

Rules: the client's result comes before anything about your company. Your company history goes at the end, if at all.
