# Template: Proposal or Quote

Most proposals open with the seller's company history and bury the client's result on page 4. The decision maker reads page 1, maybe page 2, and the price. So page 1 has to do the selling.

## The structure (page 1)

```
1. THE RESULT       What they'll have, by when, in their numbers
2. TODAY            Where they are now (their real numbers, from your conversation)
3. THE GOAL         Where they'll be, with the 1 to 3 numbers you'll track
4. HOW              3 steps, 1 line each
5. PROOF            A similar real result, or a strong process guarantee
6. INVESTMENT       The price, and what it includes, clearly (or a link to the price page)
7. NEXT STEP        One action with a date
```

Company history, team bios and terms go at the end, for the people who want them.

## Fill in version

```
[Client name]: [the result] by [date]

In [time frame], you'll have [the result, in their words and numbers].

Where you are today
- [Their number 1, for example "about 2,000 website visits a month"]
- [Their number 2, for example "3 enquiries a month"]
- [Their pain in their words, for example "you said the team spends Mondays chasing leads"]

Where you'll be
- [Goal number 1, clearly labelled as a goal]
- [Goal number 2]
- [What they'll see every week: "a 1 page report every Friday"]

How we'll get there
1. [Weeks 1 to 2: what happens]
2. [Weeks 3 to 6: what happens]
3. [Weeks 7 to 12: what happens]

Why you can trust this plan
[A similar real client result, with permission, or "If we don't hit [milestone] by [date], [what you'll do]" (only if you'll honour it)]

Investment
[Price] [per month / one time], including [the 3 most important things].
[Option B if you offer 2 options, one line.]

Next step
[One action with a date: "Reply 'approved' by Friday the 24th and we start Monday the 27th."]
```

## Line by line guidance

- **Use their numbers.** The numbers they told you on the call are the most convincing thing in the document. Write them down during the call.
- **Goals are goals, not promises.** Write "Our goal for you: 15 enquiries a month". Never "You will get 15 enquiries".
- **3 steps, not 30.** A detailed timeline can go in an appendix.
- **Price clearly, once.** Hiding the price makes people suspicious. Give 2 options at most: more options means slower decisions.
- **A dated next step.** "Reply 'approved' by Friday" makes a decision easy. Only use a real date, with a real reason if you mention one ("so we can start before your busy season").

## Common mistakes

- "About us" on page 1.
- A list of every service you offer.
- A price hidden behind "investment varies".
- 3 options plus add ons plus a custom option.
- Ending with "Please let us know if you have any questions".
