# Template: WhatsApp, Instagram or Email Reply to an Enquiry

```
Hi [name]! [Answer their question first, with a real price range or time if you can.]
[1 line of proof or reassurance: how fast, how many times you've done it, or a guarantee you really give]
[ONE next step: "Send me a photo and your area, and I'll give you the exact price."]
```

Rules: answer the question in the first line. Never reply only "please share your requirement". Under 50 words.
