# Template: Replies to Enquiries (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Email)

When someone messages "price?" or "do you do X?", they've usually messaged 2 or 3 businesses at once. The first clear, helpful answer wins. "Please share your requirement" loses.

## The structure

```
LINE 1   Answer their question (a price, a range, a yes, a time)
LINE 2   One line of reassurance or proof
LINE 3   One next step that moves to a booking, asking for only what you need
```

Under 50 words. No long greetings. Use their name if you have it.

## Fill in versions

### "How much?"
```
Hi [name]! [Service] is usually [real range], depending on [the 1 thing that changes the price].
[Proof or reassurance: "Most jobs take under an hour" / "Price confirmed before we start"]
Send me [the 1 or 2 details you need: a photo, size, date] and I'll give you the exact price [today].
```

### "Do you do X?"
```
Yes, we do [X]! [One line about how: "Usually done in 2 days, at your place or ours."]
[Proof: "We did 30 of these last month" / a review line]
[Next step: "What date were you thinking?" / "Send me a photo and I'll tell you what it needs."]
```

### "Are you available on [date]?"
```
[Yes, we have [time] free on [date] / We're full that day, but [nearest alternative].]
[Reassurance: what's included or how it works.]
Reply YES and I'll book [time] for you.
```

### "Can you give a discount?"
```
I can't lower the price for [service], because [honest reason: "we use the same 2 trained staff every time"].
What I can do: [a real alternative: smaller package, off peak slot, add on included].
Want me to book that for you?
```

### No reply after your answer (follow up once, after 1 day)
```
Hi [name], just checking: should I hold [the slot / the price] for you, or is now not a good time?
```

## Quick replies to save on your phone

Save your best 5 answers as quick replies (WhatsApp Business: Settings, Business tools, Quick replies). Then every customer gets your best answer in 2 seconds.

1. `/price` your most asked price answer
2. `/book` how to book, with the link
3. `/where` address, map link, parking
4. `/hours` opening hours
5. `/follow` the 1 day follow up

## Common mistakes

- "Please share your requirement" (makes them work before you've helped).
- "DM for price" in a public comment, then a slow reply.
- A voice note to a stranger (many people won't play it).
- Sending a full price list PDF instead of answering their question.
- Not replying for hours. Speed is proof.
