# Template: Google Business Profile (and Other Listings)

Your Google profile is often the first thing a local customer sees, before your website. They see your name, rating, photos, hours and the first lines of your description, and decide in seconds.

## The description (up to 750 characters)

### Structure
```
LINE 1   What the customer gets + the words people search ("dentist in Pune")
LINE 2   What makes it easy or fast (hours, booking, delivery, response time)
LINE 3   Real proof (years, customers served, a specific detail)
LINE 4   One next step (call, book online, order on WhatsApp)
```

### Fill in version
```
[Result for the customer] [service] in [area], [one standout detail].
[Ease: "Open 7 am to 10 pm, 7 days" / "Same day appointments" / "Free delivery within 5 km"]
[Proof: "Family run since 2009" / "1,200+ homes cleaned" / "Beans roasted every Monday"]
[Next step: "Book online in 30 seconds" / "Order ahead on WhatsApp"]
```

### Google's rules (follow them or your edit gets rejected)
- No phone numbers, links or email addresses in the description: use the profile fields for those.
- No ALL CAPS, no special characters for decoration.
- No offers or prices that change often: use Posts for those.
- Describe the business honestly. Google may remove misleading content.

## The rest of the profile (often more important than the description)

| Part | Show, don't tell version |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary category** | The most specific true category ("Cosmetic dentist" over "Dentist" if that's your main work). |
| **Photos** | Real photos of your work, place and team. Before and after shots of real jobs (with permission). Add new photos every month. |
| **Services** | Each service with a 1 line result and a price or "from" price. |
| **Products** | Your best sellers with real photos and prices. |
| **Posts** | Weekly: a recent job, a real review, a seasonal tip. Each with one next step. |
| **Questions and answers** | Add the 5 questions people ask most, with honest answers. |
| **Reviews** | Reply to every review within 2 days. Thank specifically, fix publicly if needed. |

## Replying to reviews

### Good review
```
Thank you [name]! So glad [the specific thing they mentioned] made a difference. See you next [time / season].
```

### Bad review (calm, specific, takes it offline)
```
Hi [name], I'm sorry [the specific problem]. That's not how we want anyone to feel. I've [what you did or will do]. Please call me on the number in our profile so I can make it right.
```
Never argue, never share private details, never offer money for removing a review.

## Other listings (Justdial, Yelp, Practo, Zomato, Amazon, marketplaces)

Use the same 4 line structure. Put the result and the area first, real proof next, one next step last. Keep the facts identical everywhere (name, address, hours).
