# Template: Sales Email (cold or follow up)

```
Subject: [the result, in 4 to 8 words, no hype]

Hi [first name],

[1 line: what their day looks like today, the problem in their words]
[1 line: what changes with you, the result]

[1 line of real proof: a number, a similar client, or a short quote]

[ONE ask that takes under 10 seconds: "Reply YES and I'll send...", "Is Thursday at 4 ok?"]

[Your name]
```

Rules: under 90 words. No "I hope this email finds you well". No attachments in the first email. One ask only.
